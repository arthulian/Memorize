Eight Capacities

Visual Framework
I

Cognitive Principles

Sense-Making

Deliberate skepticism of your own reasoning to avoid bias or fallacies.

Objectivity

Pre-emptively identify potential falsifiers before committing to conclusions.

Fairness

Use objective evaluation; avoid motivated reasoning.

Accuracy over Ego

Prioritize being correct over being "right."

II

Thinking Methods

Steel-man

Construct the opponent's argument in its strongest form to test survival.

Confidence Calibration

Proportion certainty to quality of evidence.

III

Decentering Self

Self-Confidence

Observe thoughts/emotions as transient phenomena or programming.

Stable Self-Worth

Rooted in moral character, integrity, and volition.

Dependent Self-Worth

Dependent on external validation and social achievements.

IV

Stress & Regulation

Distress

Reduce triggers, step back, and recover.

Optimal Stress

Use moderate stress in a productive way (Goldilocks Zone).

Action & Attribution

Build competence through practice and attribute outcomes to skill.

Formal Capacities
V

Core Definitions

Integrity

Consistent regulation of behavior that aligns actions with principles.

Sense-making

Integrating metacognitive signals to revise preconceptions.

Objectivity

Mitigates motivated reasoning through consistent self-critique.

Fairness

A proportional relationship between intent and consequence.

Intentionality

Allocate effort according to future importance rather than sunk costs.

Perseverance

Long-term persistence utilized to override fear-driven avoidance.

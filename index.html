Eight Capacities &
Critical Thinking Framework

A structured outline combining the visual framework and formal capacity definitions

From Visual Framework
I

Core Cognitive Principles

Sense-Making

Deliberate skepticism of your own reasoning to avoid bias or fallacies.

Objectivity

Pre-emptively identify potential falsifiers before committing to conclusions.

Fairness

Use objective evaluation; avoid motivated reasoning.

Accuracy over Ego

Prioritize being correct over being "right."

II

Critical Thinking Methods

Steel-man

Construct the opponent's argument in its strongest committed form, then test whether it survives that commitment set.

Confidence Calibration

Proportion certainty to quality of evidence.

III

Decentering Self

Self-Confidence

Observe thoughts/emotions as transient phenomena or subconscious programming.

Stable Self-Worth

Rooted in moral character (integrity, volition, empathy, and emotional regulation).

Dependent Self-Worth

Dependent on external validation and social achievements.

Defensive Self-Worth

Relies on performative self-reliance and independence.

IV

Stress & Regulation

Distress

Reduce triggers, step back, and recover.

Optimal Stress

Use moderate stress in a productive way (Goldilocks Zone).

1. Action

Build competence through disciplined practice of skills.

2. Attribution

Attribute outcomes to proven competence.

3. Self-Appraisal

Update confidence based on attribution.

4. Higher Threshold

Increased confidence allows tackling more complex challenges.

From Eight Capacities
V

Eight Capacities

Integrity

Consistent regulation of behavior that aligns actions with principles.

Sense-making

Converts data into structured schemas by integrating metacognitive signals.

Critical Thinking

Determines prior plausibility; distinguishes claims, evidence, and inference.

Objectivity

Mitigates motivated reasoning through consistent rules and self-critique.

Fairness

Proportional relationship between empathy (intent) and accountability (consequence).

Self-confidence

Self-appraisal of past achievements; attributes self-belief to performance.

Intentionality

Allocate effort according to future importance; minimize decision load.

Perseverance

Long-term persistence; utilizes gradual exposure to override fear.

Humility

Deliberate question your own reasoning to avoid biases or logical fallacies.

Critical Thinking

Pre-emptively spot potential falsifiers (ways conclusions could be proven wrong) before committing to these conclusions.

Fairness

Balances the degree of empathy in someone's intent with accountability for the actual consequences of their actions, using a proportional approach.

Objectivity

Prioritize being correct over winning an argument, by applying consistent standards during self-critique.

Steel-man

Construct the opponent's argument in its strongest committed form and then test whether it survives its own logic.

Confidence Calibration

Proportion certainty to quality of evidence.

Observer Mindset

Observe thoughts/emotions as transient phenomena or subconscious programming.

Stable Self-Worth

Rooted in moral character, integrity, and volition; It is not dependent on social recognition or defensive self-sufficiency

Distress

Step back to reduce triggers and recover quickly.

Optimal Stress

Use moderate pressure (in the goldilocks zone) for a productive performance.

Grounded Self-efficacy

Realistically assess your abilities and then correctly crediting successes to the appropriate causes.

Integrity

Consistent regulation of behavior that aligns actions with principles.

Sense-making

Integrating metacognitive signals to revise preconceptions.

Internal Locus of control

Allocates effort to what can be influenced moving forward instead of dwelling in sunk costs.

Perseverance

Sustain long-term effort to push through fear-driven avoidance.

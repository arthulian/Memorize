The Virtuous Loop of Agency

Integrated Framework for Cognitive Excellence & Moral Clarity

I

Intellectual Humility

Raw data is processed through a skepticism filter to convert chaos into structured schemas while removing egoic bias.

Sense-Making

Converting data into schemas by integrating metacognitive signals and revising preconceptions.

Objectivity

Identifying falsifiers pre-emptively and applying uniform empirical standards via Bayes-updating.

Steel-Manning

Constructing the strongest possible version of opposing arguments to test personal plausibility.

Accuracy Over Ego

The fundamental commitment to being correct over the comfort of being right.

II

Moral Calibration

Filtered facts are aligned with internal principles and social accountability to ensure integrity and define action.

Integrity

Consistent regulation of behavior that aligns actions with principles and moral commitment.

Fairness

Balancing empathy with accountability through objective evaluation.

Internal Locus of Control

Allocating effort based on future importance; minimizing sunk-cost bias and decision load.

Stable Self-Worth

Anchoring the foundation of identity in moral character rather than external validation.

III

The Action Engine

Belief is converted into results through a performance-psychology feedback loop to drive long-term persistence.

Perseverance

Long-term persistence of action that overrides fear-driven avoidance through exposure.

Self-Confidence

Self-appraisal of past achievements, viewing setbacks as temporary skill gaps.

The Competence-Confidence Loop

1
Action: Build competence through disciplined skill practice.

2
Attribution: Link outcomes to proven competence.

3
Appraisal: Update self-belief based on demonstrated performance.

4
Threshold: Tackling higher challenges, restarting the cycle.

IV

Regulatory Maintenance

Monitors the Goldilocks Zone of stress to maintain peak performance and prevent system collapse.

Stress Optimization

Identifying the difference between Distress and Optimal Stress to regulate recovery and effort.

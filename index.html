Glossary

01

Self-Reflection

Deliberately question your own reasoning to avoid biases or logical fallacies.

02

Critical Thinking

Pre-emptively spot potential falsifiers (ways conclusions could be proven wrong) before committing to those conclusions.

03

Fairness

Proportionately balance empathy for someone's intent with accountability for the actual consequences of their actions.

04

Objectivity

Prioritize being correct over winning an argument by applying consistent standards during self-critique.

05

Steel-man

Construct the opponent's argument in its strongest committed form and then test whether it survives its own logic.

06

Confidence Calibration

Proportion certainty to the quality of evidence.

07

Observer Mindset

Observe thoughts and emotions as transient phenomena or subconscious programming.

08

Stable Self-Worth

Rooted in moral character, integrity, and volition. It is not dependent on social recognition or defensive self-sufficiency.

09

Distress

Step back to reduce triggers and recover quickly.

10

Optimal Stress

Use moderate pressure within the Goldilocks zone for productive performance.

11

Grounded Self-Efficacy

Realistically assess your abilities and correctly credit successes to the appropriate causes.

12

Integrity

Consistent regulation of behavior that aligns actions with principles.

13

Sense-Making

Integrate metacognitive signals to revise preconceptions.

14

Internal Locus of Control

Allocate effort toward what can be influenced moving forward instead of dwelling on sunk costs.

15

Perseverance

Sustain long-term effort to push through fear-driven avoidance.

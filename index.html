Glossary

Self-Reflection

Consciously scrutinize your own reasoning to avoid biases or logical fallacies.

Critical Thinking

Pre-emptively spot potential falsifiers, before committing to any particular conclusions.

Fairness

Proportionately balance someone's intent by the empathy within it, with accountability for the consequences of their actions.

Objectivity

Prioritize being correct over winning an argument by applying consistent standards during self-critique.

Steelmanning

Construct the opponent's argument in its strongest committed form and then test whether it survives its own logic.

Confidence Calibration

Proportion certainty to the quality of knowledge or evidence.

Observer Mindset

Observe thoughts and emotions as transient phenomena or subconscious programming.

Stable Self-Worth

Rooted in moral character, integrity, and volition. It is not dependent on social recognition or defensive self-sufficiency.

Distress

Under overwhelming pressure; step back to reduce triggers and allow recovery.

Optimal Stress

Under moderate pressure (the Goldilocks zone); aim for productive performance.

Grounded Self-Efficacy

Realistically assess your capacity to perform and then correctly credit successes to the corresponding causes.

Integrity

Internal consistency by using self-moderation to align desires with core values; ensuring actions remain uncompromisingly locked to those core values.

Internal Locus of Control

Allocates effort towards upcoming events that can be influenced, rather than dwelling on sunk costs that can't be recovered.

Perseverance

Sustains long-term effort with the courage to push through fear-driven avoidance.

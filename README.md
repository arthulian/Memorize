# Specification Framework

## Purpose

Provide a coherent personal operating framework for perceiving reality accurately, reasoning soundly, regulating oneself effectively, maintaining a principled identity, and acting wisely through continuous learning and moral integrity.

---

# 0. Legend

**Truthfulness** — Definition: Alignment of beliefs, representations, and actions with reality as best understood. | **Requires:** Belief Updating. | **Governs:** Perception • Reasoning • Identity • Action. | **Constrains:** Self-Deception. | **Produces:** Accurate Representation.

**Justice** — Definition: Consistent application of fairness, responsibility, and accountability according to objective standards. | **Requires:** Truthfulness • Wisdom. | **Governs:** Reasoning • Action. | **Constrains:** Bias • Partiality. | **Produces:** Fair Judgment.

**Compassion** — Definition: Recognition of suffering coupled with a commitment to reduce unnecessary harm while preserving responsibility. | **Requires:** Understanding. | **Governs:** Self-Regulation • Action. | **Constrains:** Cruelty • Indifference. | **Produces:** Benevolent Action.

**Wisdom** — Definition: Disciplined application of knowledge, experience, and judgment toward beneficial action. | **Requires:** Truthfulness • Reflection. | **Governs:** Reasoning • Self-Regulation • Action. | **Produces:** Sound Judgment.

**Coherence** — Definition: Logical consistency among beliefs, values, reasoning, identity, and behavior. | **Requires:** Truthfulness. | **Governs:** Reasoning • Identity. | **Evaluates:** Internal Consistency. | **Produces:** Alignment.

**Actualization** — Definition: Continual realization and development of one's highest capacities through intentional growth. | **Requires:** Reflection • Learning • Action. | **Governs:** Continuous Improvement. | **Produces:** Development.

**Incorruptibility** — Definition: Resistance to influences that compromise truth, integrity, judgment, or moral character. | **Requires:** Truthfulness • Coherence. | **Governs:** Reasoning • Identity • Action. | **Constrains:** Corruption • Rationalization. | **Produces:** Integrity.

---

# I. Perception

**Perception** — Definition: Acquisition and interpretation of information about reality. | **Governed By:** Truthfulness • Wisdom. | **Requires:** Attention • Observation • Curiosity. | **Uses:** Credibility Calibration • Belief Updating • Stress & Bias Mitigation. | **Processes:** Observe → Compare Sources → Evaluate Credibility → Detect Bias → Update Beliefs. | **Produces:** Evidence • Situational Awareness • Updated Beliefs. | **Feeds Into:** Reasoning.

---

# II. Reasoning

**Reasoning** — Definition: Evaluation and integration of information to produce justified conclusions. | **Governed By:** Truthfulness • Wisdom • Coherence • Incorruptibility. | **Requires:** Evidence • Updated Beliefs • Context. | **Uses:** Robust Evaluation • Uncorrupted Judgment • Contextual Empathy. | **Processes:** Interpret → Analyze → Compare → Test Assumptions → Integrate Context → Form Conclusions. | **Produces:** Understanding • Decisions • Intentions. | **Feeds Into:** Self-Regulation • Action.

---

# III. Self-Regulation

**Self-Regulation** — Definition: Management of thoughts, emotions, and behavior in service of principled action. | **Governed By:** Wisdom • Compassion • Actualization. | **Requires:** Self-Awareness • Reflection • Reasoning. | **Uses:** Grounded Awareness • Acceptance & Recovery • Calibrated Stress Response. | **Processes:** Monitor → Regulate Emotion → Recover → Recalibrate. | **Produces:** Emotional Stability • Resilience • Behavioral Readiness. | **Feeds Into:** Identity • Action.

---

# IV. Identity

**Identity** — Definition: Integrated and enduring organization of one's beliefs, values, character, and self-concept. | **Governed By:** Coherence • Compassion • Incorruptibility. | **Requires:** Reflection • Experience • Self-Regulation. | **Uses:** Anchored Self-Worth • Calibrated Confidence • Failure as Feedback. | **Processes:** Reflect → Integrate Experience → Align Values → Preserve Integrity. | **Produces:** Stable Identity • Principled Character. | **Feeds Into:** Action.

---

# V. Action

**Action** — Definition: Intentional execution of decisions within the external world. | **Governed By:** Truthfulness • Wisdom • Coherence • Actualization. | **Requires:** Decisions • Stable Identity • Self-Regulation. | **Uses:** Execution Loop • Strategic Focus • Sustainable Persistence • Flexible Commitment. | **Processes:** Plan → Prioritize → Execute → Monitor Outcomes → Adjust Strategy. | **Produces:** Outcomes • Experience • Consequences. | **Feeds Into:** Continuous Improvement.

---

# VI. Continuous Improvement

**Continuous Improvement** — Definition: Continual refinement through evidence, reflection, and adaptation. | **Governed By:** Truthfulness • Wisdom • Actualization. | **Requires:** Outcomes • Evidence • Reflection. | **Uses:** Feedback • Learning • Adaptation • Deliberate Practice. | **Processes:** Evaluate Outcomes → Identify Errors → Update Beliefs → Refine Strategies → Reinforce Effective Practices. | **Produces:** Improved Perception • Improved Reasoning • Improved Self-Regulation • Improved Identity • Improved Action. | **Feeds Into:** Perception • Reasoning • Self-Regulation • Identity • Action.
